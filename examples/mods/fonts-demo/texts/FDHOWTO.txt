^P
^C0A
How the Fonts Demo is made
^C00

fonts.yaml names each font and what
it is made of: Wolf3D's SmallFont or
LargeFont (type: vga), a font file,
a picture sheet or a picture a
character. Shadows, gradients,
outlines and glows go on any font.

Text is printed by font name: menu
Labels, menu items, the messages
over the view, Lua's game.drawtext.
^P
^C0A
Where it all is
^C00

menudefs/ holds these pages, and
scripts/fonts-demo.lua prints fonts
over the view as you play. Type
"fonts" in the console to hide them.

The pictures are drawn by
examples/tools/make-fonts-demo.ps1.
