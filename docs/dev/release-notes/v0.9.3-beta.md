## Changed

- **Blake Stone: Aliens of Gold plays v3.0 by default, and v1.0, v2.1 and v3.0 all work.** The three
  releases' data files have the same names (`VGAGRAPH.BS6` and the rest), and PFWolf now tells them
  apart by their contents and picks the right one: v3.0 (`--game blake`), v1.0 (`blake-v1`) or
  v2.1 (`blake-v2`, the version GOG sells). Before, only v1.0's files were right: v2.1's and v3.0's
  showed the wrong pictures in the menus, help pages and status bar. You don't need to choose: with
  `--game blake`, or no `--game`, it plays whichever release's files you have. Each release keeps
  its own title screen, help pages, ordering info and menu colors; v3.0's ordering info has the
  Planet Strike adverts.
- **Blake Stone's shareware (v3.0) high score table** gives Apogee's 1-800 number, as the original
  does.

## For mod makers

- **`blake` is now Aliens of Gold v3.0.** The `blake` pack's `raw-data-map.yaml` names v3.0's
  VGAGRAPH, its `presenter.yaml` has shapes 214-218 (the L and Shift keys, the Planet Strike
  adverts), its `colors.yaml` clears the menus with v3.0's lighter terminal (`MENUBACK`) and its
  high score table gives v3.0's order line. The new `blake-v1` and `blake-v2` packs lay their own
  over these. The pictures and texts keep the names they had: only the chunks behind them moved.
- **A game a mod adds on `base-pack: blake`** without a `file-pack` of its own now reads the data
  files with v3.0's layout. If it's meant for v1.0's files, make it `base-pack: blake-v1`.

## Compatibility

- **Saves:** Aliens of Gold saves stay where they were (the `BlakeStone` folder) and load in any of
  the three releases. A save from v1.0 loaded with v2.1's or v3.0's files plays on their maps,
  which differ on a few floors.
- **Multiplayer:** everyone needs the same Aliens of Gold release.
