## Changed

- **Blake Stone: Aliens of Gold plays v3.0 by default, and v1.0, v2.1 and v3.0 all work.** The three
  releases' data files have the same names (`VGAGRAPH.BS6` and the rest), and PFWolf now tells them
  apart by their contents and picks the right one: v3.0 (`--game blake`), v1.0 (`blake-v1`) or
  v2.1 (`blake-v2`, the version GOG sells). Before, only v1.0's files were right: v2.1's and v3.0's
  showed the wrong pictures in the menus, help pages and status bar. You don't need to choose: with
  `--game blake`, or no `--game`, it plays whichever release's files you have. Each release keeps
  its own title screen, help pages, ordering info and menu colors; v3.0's ordering info has the
  Planet Strike adverts.
- **Blake Stone's shareware (v3.0) high score table** gives Apogee's 1-800 number, as the original
  does.
- **Vehicles work in multiplayer.** Other players see someone riding a jeep, tank or boat as the
  vehicle itself, not as a player on foot. In a game against each other, running into a rival at
  speed hurts them (a frag if it kills); a teammate is never hit. A rider who's killed leaves the
  vehicle's wreck behind, and another of the same vehicle comes back later on one of the map's
  spots for it. Wrecked vehicles also come back in Horde games, where the players are on one side.

## Fixed

- **Using a vehicle or a mounted gun in a network game** put each machine's own player in the
  seat, not the player who pressed use, and the game fell out of step. Anything a player uses now
  acts as that player on every machine.
- **A player killed while riding in a network game** came back still riding, and the vehicle was
  gone for the rest of the match.
- **Someone joining a network game mid-match** wasn't told who was riding what.

## For mod makers

- **`blake` is now Aliens of Gold v3.0.** The `blake` pack's `raw-data-map.yaml` names v3.0's
  VGAGRAPH, its `presenter.yaml` has shapes 214-218 (the L and Shift keys, the Planet Strike
  adverts), its `colors.yaml` clears the menus with v3.0's lighter terminal (`MENUBACK`) and its
  high score table gives v3.0's order line. The new `blake-v1` and `blake-v2` packs lay their own
  over these. The pictures and texts keep the names they had: only the chunks behind them moved.
- **A game a mod adds on `base-pack: blake`** without a `file-pack` of its own now reads the data
  files with v3.0's layout. If it's meant for v1.0's files, make it `base-pack: blake-v1`.
- **Vehicles have two new properties** (new fields only; existing vehicles are unchanged):
  `vehicle.wreck`, a class put down where a rider is killed, and `vehicle.respawntics`, how long
  after a wreck another of the vehicle comes back in a game against each other (default 2100, or
  30 seconds; -1 for never). A vehicle can also have **riding states**, which other players see
  a rider through: `Ride` (still), `RideSee` (moving), `RideMissile` (firing) and `RidePain`. A
  vehicle without them is seen in its `Spawn` look. `vehicle.crushdamage` now hurts rival
  players too.
- **A vehicle can show who's riding it** (a new field only; existing vehicles are unchanged):
  `vehicle.ridersprites` maps player classes to sprites drawn in place of the vehicle's own
  while that class rides it, so a Guard can be seen at the wheel of a jeep and B.J. in a tank's
  hatch. A class not listed uses its parent's entry; a frame the sprite lacks shows the vehicle.
  See "Player classes" in the manual.
- **Things that stand still can be seen from 8 sides.** A decoration or a parked vehicle whose
  sprite has views (`A1`-`A8`) now shows the side you see it from, by the way the map faces it.
  Before, it always showed its front. With `angles: -1` it still shows its front to everyone. A
  vehicle drawn from 8 sides keeps its facing: left behind facing the way its rider faced, and
  brought back in a deathmatch facing as the map has it. Getting into one turns you to face its
  way. Vehicles seen only from the front work as before.
- **A map can have its own Horde waves** (a new field only; existing maps are unchanged): a
  `horde:` block in a map's `game-info.yaml` entry, laid out as the top-level one, is used on
  that map and leaves the game's waves for every other map. A map pack can bring a Horde arena
  with its own enemies without changing Horde on anyone else's maps. A game with no waves of its
  own (Spear) offers Horde in the lobby when such an arena is loaded.

## Compatibility

- **Saves:** Aliens of Gold saves stay where they were (the `BlakeStone` folder) and load in any of
  the three releases. A save from v1.0 loaded with v2.1's or v3.0's files plays on their maps,
  which differ on a few floors.
- **Multiplayer:** everyone needs the same Aliens of Gold release. Everyone also needs 0.9.3: a
  0.9.2 player can't join a 0.9.3 game mid-match (the game state sent to them has changed).
