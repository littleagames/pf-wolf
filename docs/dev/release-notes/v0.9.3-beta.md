**PFWolf** (Project-Friendly Wolfenstein 3D) is a source port of Wolfenstein 3-D, Spear of Destiny
and Blake Stone, built so you can make a game, or change one, with data files instead of
changing the engine. It comes with **PFWolf Editor**, a map and content editor for the same files.

0.9.3 brings Spear of Destiny's bonus screens and several Spear and Wolfenstein 3-D fixes,
support for every release of Blake Stone: Aliens of Gold, vehicles in multiplayer, objective
markers, and sound that changes from room to room. New to PFWolf? The
[0.9.1 beta notes](https://github.com/littleagames/pf-wolf/releases/tag/v0.9.1-beta) cover
everything in it and which game data files it needs.

Each has the game, the editor and the example mods. To update, replace the old folder (or app)
with the new one. Your saves, settings and mods live in your PFWolf data folder, so they stay where
they are.

## The first run

- **Windows:** unzip anywhere and run `PFWolf.exe` (the editor is `PFWolf.Editor.exe`). The
  programs aren't signed yet, so SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": choose
  **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** untar anywhere and run `./PFWolf`; `./install-desktop-entries.sh` adds the game and
  the editor to your application menu. Or make the AppImage executable and run it: it's the game,
  and `--editor` opens the editor.
- **macOS:** unzip, then drag **PFWolf.app** and **PFWolf Editor.app** into Applications. They
  aren't notarized yet, so the first time macOS won't open them: right-click the app and choose
  **Open** (on macOS 15 and later: try once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open
  Anyway**). In the editor, choose PFWolf.app as the game folder.

## Changed

- **Blake Stone: Aliens of Gold plays v3.0 by default, and v1.0, v2.1 and v3.0 all work.** The three
  releases' data files have the same names (`VGAGRAPH.BS6` and the rest), and PFWolf now tells them
  apart by their contents and picks the right one: v3.0 (`--game blake`), v1.0 (`blake-v1`) or
  v2.1 (`blake-v2`, the version GOG sells). Before, only v1.0's files were right: v2.1's and v3.0's
  showed the wrong pictures in the menus, help pages and status bar. You don't need to choose: with
  `--game blake`, or no `--game`, it plays whichever release's files you have. Each release keeps
  its own title screen, help pages, ordering info and menu colors; v3.0's ordering info has the
  Planet Strike adverts.
- **Blake Stone's shareware (v3.0) high score table** gives Apogee's 1-800 number, as the original
  does.
- **Vehicles work in multiplayer.** Other players see someone riding a jeep, tank or boat as the
  vehicle itself, not as a player on foot. In a game against each other, running into a rival at
  speed hurts them (a frag if it kills); a teammate is never hit. A rider who's killed leaves the
  vehicle's wreck behind, and another of the same vehicle comes back later on one of the map's
  spots for it. Wrecked vehicles also come back in Horde games, where the players are on one side.
- **Objective markers.** King of the Hill, Domination and Capture the Flag show where their
  objectives are: a diamond over the spot, seen through walls, with a label and the distance, or
  an arrow at the view's edge when it's off to the side or behind, in its team's color. The
  automap shows them too. In Horde the last few enemies are marked. A level's objectives (and a
  mod's scripts) can mark things in single player and co-op as well. **Game Options → Objective
  Markers** turns them off.
- **Secret floors and Spear's bonus screens.** Wolfenstein 3-D's secret floors end on the "secret
  floor completed!" screen with its 15000 bonus, not the regular tally. Spear of Destiny's boss
  floors and secret areas end on their own screens ("trans grosse defeated!", "secret tunnel area
  completed!" and the rest). A bonus floor's kills, secrets, treasure and time are kept for the
  episode, and left out of the victory tally as in the original.
- **Sound that changes from room to room** (used by the new Ambience Demo mod; stock games sound
  as before):
  - **Options → Sound → Reverb**: rooms can have their own echo (a cave, a sewer pipe, a
    stone hall), crossfading as you walk between them. On by default, and only heard where a map
    or mod sets a reverb.
  - **Game Options → Muffle Thru Doors**: sounds from behind shut doors are quieter and duller,
    and swell as a door opens. Off by default.
- **Control → Mouse Walk** (Wolfenstein 3-D and Blake Stone): turn it off and moving the mouse
  up and down no longer walks you forward and back when mouse look is off.
- **The Mods menu scrolls** instead of paging, and every menu takes **PgUp, PgDn, Home and End**.
  The mod catalog moved off the list: open it with Tab, the middle mouse button or a
  controller's X.
- **The skill and class menus fit any number of skills and classes**, so a mod with five skills
  or seven classes no longer runs off the window.
- **The rocket launcher's blast** (multiplayer) reaches 3 tiles and does 50-120 damage, falling
  off with distance (was 30-90 within 1 tile).
- **More ammo out in the open on The Wheel (DM03)**, and no ceiling lights hanging from the open
  sky on Hell's Highways (DM02).

## Fixed

### Playing

- **Wolfenstein 3-D's E5 secret elevator** goes to E5M10, E6M10 sends you back to the right
  floor, and finishing E6M10 no longer ends the episode.
- **Spear of Destiny:**
  - **Spectres** come back after being shot, as in the original, and aren't counted as kills (so
    the kill ratio can't pass 100%).
  - **The Spear pickup's sound** plays to its end before the next floor.
  - **B.J.'s collapse** at the end runs its full length (about 8 seconds, not 4.5).
- **The Pac-Man ghosts** are drawn where they are. Before, they jumped a tile at a time, showed up
  a tile ahead of themselves and seemed to touch you without hurting you.
- **Breaking a wall beside a door** takes the door out too. Before, the door stood edge-on in the
  hole.
- **Allies and prisoners:**
  - **An idle ally or prisoner in a doorway** steps out of the way when you walk into it, so it
    can no longer block your way.
  - **A dead ally or prisoner stays down.** Before, its body stood back up once the last enemy
    died.
  - **A prisoner you use as it steps aside** stops, instead of gliding on in its standing frame.
  - **Allies no longer walk through you.**
- **Hub levels:** going back into a level you left by its elevator found the switch dead and left
  you stuck; it's put back. A plain picked-up item (`CustomInventory`) is now kept, so a hub's
  collected items come back with you.
- **An exit refused by a level objective** (an elevator used too early) left the switch thrown
  and dead, and a key the switch takes was used up. The switch now stays usable and the key stays
  yours.
- **The ammo counter** on Wolfenstein 3-D's status bar showed the last weapon's ammo after
  switching between weapons with different ammo (the multiplayer rocket launcher and
  flamethrower).

### Multiplayer

- **A crash on Hell's Highways (DM02)**: a flame bursting inside the map's outer wall closed the
  game on every machine.
- **Using a vehicle or a mounted gun in a network game** put each machine's own player in the
  seat, not the player who pressed use, and the game fell out of step. Anything a player uses now
  acts as that player on every machine.
- **A player killed while riding in a network game** came back still riding, and the vehicle was
  gone for the rest of the match.
- **Someone joining a network game mid-match** wasn't told who was riding what.
- **The lobby's highlight bar** lines up with its row's text.

### Example mods

- **Episode Demo:** the Docks' doors stand the right way, and the Tower's ending can be reached.
- **Weather Demo:** the elevator switches are on walls you can use, and the porch lamps hang from
  the roof.
- **Pits Demo** is back, rebuilt with story heights, with a proper elevator at the exit.
- **Mission Demo** has a second floor with a locked exit; **Movement Demo** (ice, mud, conveyors,
  kick pads, all in Lua) and **Ambience Demo** (zone sounds, placed sounds, reverb, muffling) are
  new.

## PFWolf Editor

- **Menus window** (Resources → Menus…, Ctrl+Shift+M): every menu the game uses, drawn as the
  game draws it. Edit a menu's fields or drag its parts on the picture, save the changes into a
  mod, compare with the stock menu, save the picture as a PNG, and **Test in game** (F5) to open
  it in the game. The F11 Definitions window previews a menudefs file's menu as you type.
- **Sound:** the actor window has a loop sound per frame, and Map Properties has a map's reverb
  and each zone's reverb, second ambient sound and fade.

## For mod makers

Nothing a mod is made of has changed incompatibly, except the `blake` base pack below. Everything
else is new fields; mods made for 0.9.2 work as they did.

- **`blake` is now Aliens of Gold v3.0.** The `blake` pack's `raw-data-map.yaml` names v3.0's
  VGAGRAPH, its `presenter.yaml` has shapes 214-218 (the L and Shift keys, the Planet Strike
  adverts), its `colors.yaml` clears the menus with v3.0's lighter terminal (`MENUBACK`) and its
  high score table gives v3.0's order line. The new `blake-v1` and `blake-v2` packs lay their own
  over these. The pictures and texts keep the names they had: only the chunks behind them moved.
  **A game a mod adds on `base-pack: blake`** without a `file-pack` of its own now reads the data
  files with v3.0's layout. If it's meant for v1.0's files, make it `base-pack: blake-v1`.
- **Vehicles:** `vehicle.wreck` (a class put down where a rider is killed),
  `vehicle.respawntics` (how long until another comes back in a game against each other; default
  2100, or 30 seconds; -1 for never), riding states seen by other players (`Ride`, `RideSee`,
  `RideMissile`, `RidePain`; without them a vehicle is seen in its `Spawn` look), and
  `vehicle.ridersprites`, which maps player classes to sprites drawn while that class rides it (a
  class not listed uses its parent's). `vehicle.crushdamage` now hurts rival players too.
- **Things that stand still can be seen from 8 sides.** A decoration or parked vehicle whose
  sprite has views (`A1`-`A8`) shows the side you see it from, by the way the map faces it. With
  `angles: -1` it still shows its front to everyone.
- **A map can have its own Horde waves:** a `horde:` block in a map's `game-info.yaml` entry,
  laid out as the top-level one. A game with no waves of its own (Spear) offers Horde in the
  lobby when such an arena is loaded.
- **Objective markers:** `game-info.yaml` `objective-markers` (colors, flash, distance, font),
  `marker: true` (with `marker-label`, `marker-color`) on level objectives, `horde` `mark-last`,
  and in Lua `game.marker(id, x, y, label[, color])`, `game.marker(id, actor, label[, color])`
  and `game.clearmarker([id])`. Markers aren't saved: set them in `game.onlevel`.
- **Bonus screens:** a `game-info.yaml` map's `bonus-screen: true` ends it on `intermission.yaml`'s `bonus-screen`, with `bonus-screen-labels` for its
  text; a cluster's `victory-counts-bonus-floors: true` counts those floors in the victory tally.
- **Sound:**
  - Zones: `ambient-fade`, `ambient-2` (with `-disguised` and `-undetected`), `reverb`; maps:
    `reverb`, `reverb-fade`. A reverb is one of the EFX preset names (`cave`, `sewer-pipe`,
    `stone-room`…). `game-info.yaml` `sound-muffle` (0.35) sets how much a shut door takes off.
  - `sounds/sound-seq.yaml` entries: `volume`, `volume-variance`, `pitch`, `pitch-variance`,
    `reverb` (0 keeps a sound dry).
  - actordefs: `loopsound` and `loopradius` on a class, `loop-sound:` on a frame.
  - Lua: `game.playsound(sound[, x, y])`, `game.ambient(sound, tics[, layer])` and
    `game.reverb(preset[, tics])`; each holds over the zones until it's given `nil`.
- **Lua `actor:move(dx, dy)`** pushes a player or monster by up to 4 tiles and returns how far it
  went: see the Movement Demo's `movement.lua`.
- **Menus:** menudefs `fit-items` (with `min-rows`, `max-rows`) grows or shrinks a menu's window to
  its number of items; `selection-pic` takes `width`, `height` and `color`; the `menu NAME`
  console command opens a menu for testing.
- **`CustomInventory`** pickups without a `Pickup` state are now kept (up to their max), not
  only taken off the floor.
- **New console commands:** `menu`, `ambient`, `loops`, `reverb`, `soundinfo`; new settings
  `m_walk`, `hud_markers`, `s_muffle`, `s_reverb`.

## Compatibility

- **Saves from 0.9.2 load in 0.9.3.** Aliens of Gold saves stay where they were (the
  `BlakeStone` folder) and load in any of the three releases. A save from v1.0 loaded with v2.1's
  or v3.0's files plays on their maps, which differ on a few floors.
- **In a multiplayer game everyone needs the same version:** 0.9.2 and 0.9.3 can't play together.
  Everyone also needs the same Aliens of Gold release.

## Known issues

<!-- decide: add or remove anything you know of from testing -->
- **The programs aren't signed:** SmartScreen asks on Windows, and Gatekeeper on a Mac (see
  [The first run](#the-first-run)).
- **The editor on a Mac uses the Control key**, not Command, for its shortcuts.
- **Reverb and muffling need OpenAL's EFX**, which the bundled OpenAL Soft has. Without it the
  sounds play dry, at full treble.
- **With the uncapped frame rate,** the view and actors move smoothly, but sliding doors,
  pushwalls and the weapon bob still move in the game's 70-a-second steps.
- **Blake Stone and Planet Strike** are close to the originals, but a few behaviours still
  differ.
