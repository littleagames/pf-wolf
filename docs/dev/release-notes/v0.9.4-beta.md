**PFWolf** (Project-Friendly Wolfenstein 3D) is a source port of Wolfenstein 3-D, Spear of Destiny
and Blake Stone, built so you can make a game, or change one, with data files instead of
changing the engine. It comes with **PFWolf Editor**, a map and content editor for the same files.

0.9.4 is mostly Blake Stone: its score bonuses, enemies that see and hear you as they did in DOS,
pushwalls at their own speed, and canisters and crates that stay in the way. It also fixes
enemies jumping back through doors, and flats in a few example mods. New to PFWolf? The
[0.9.1 beta notes](https://github.com/littleagames/pf-wolf/releases/tag/v0.9.1-beta) cover
everything in it and which game data files it needs.

Each has the game, the editor and the example mods. To update, replace the old folder (or app)
with the new one. Your saves, settings and mods live in your PFWolf data folder, so they stay where
they are.

## The first run

- **Windows:** unzip anywhere and run `PFWolf.exe` (the editor is `PFWolf.Editor.exe`). The
  programs aren't signed yet, so SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": choose
  **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** untar anywhere and run `./PFWolf`; `./install-desktop-entries.sh` adds the game and
  the editor to your application menu. Or make the AppImage executable and run it: it's the game,
  and `--editor` opens the editor.
- **macOS:** unzip, then drag **PFWolf.app** and **PFWolf Editor.app** into Applications. They
  aren't notarized yet, so the first time macOS won't open them: right-click the app and choose
  **Open** (on macOS 15 and later: try once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open
  Anyway**). In the editor, choose PFWolf.app as the game folder.

## Changed

- **Blake Stone's bonuses** (Aliens of Gold and Planet Strike), which were missing:
  - Each floor's **ALL ENEMY DESTROYED!**, **ALL POINTS COLLECTED!** and **ALL INFORMANTS
    ALIVE!**, 50,000 points each, in the info area with their sound.
  - **An extra life every 400,000 points**, up to 4 extra lives, as the original (before, you
    could hold 9).
  - **GREAT SCORE!** at 500,000 and **ROLLED SCORE DISPLAY!** past 9,999,999 (with "rOLL" on the
    score display): full ammo, full health and 1,000,000 points.
  - Aliens of Gold: destroying a **guardian alien** tells you to find the exit (on mission 6, to
    destroy the projection generators).
  - A bonus stays up in the info area for 7 seconds: picking things up meanwhile doesn't wipe it.

## Fixed

### Playing

- **Enemies going through a door they waited at** no longer jump back a tile once it's open
  (mostly seen in Blake Stone).
- **Blake Stone and Planet Strike:**
  - **Enemies see you across a room that spans several floor codes** without a door between.
    Before, they stood still and blind until gunfire woke them.
  - **Enemies hear gunfire** whichever order they're in on the map. Before, a rent-a-cop next to
    others shooting at you could patrol on, deaf.
  - **Pushwalls slide at the original speed**, four times Wolfenstein 3-D's.
  - **Alien canisters, gurneys, pod eggs and crates** block only their own tile, so you can walk
    close past them (on Aliens of Gold mission 4, floor 1, a large canister caught you a row
    away). **Broken canisters, empty gurneys and morphing posts stay solid**: you can't walk
    through them, and a volatile transport no longer drives into one and blows up.
  - **A volatile transport parked facing a wall** stays parked, instead of starting inside the
    wall and blowing up on the canister beside it.
  - **Barriers switched back on with an enemy in them** hurt it and close again. Before, the tile
    stayed open for good.
  - **Electro-spheres** zap a third as often, and **electro-aliens** fire five bolts a burst, as
    the DOS game played. Before, a sphere killed you in under a second.
  - **Distant colors in dark areas** no longer show bright green or white, or pulse while the
    popup map is open.
- **Walls taller than the level under a sky** no longer draw over the ceiling flats.
- **Doorways between roofed rooms** show the plain floor color or sky, not a smear of the
  neighbouring flat.

### Example mods

- **Doorways in seven example mods** (Architecture, Detonator, Episode, Mission, Rooftops,
  Vehicles, Wall Sprites and Weather Demos) take their room's floor and ceiling, instead of bare
  floor and sky.
- **Mission Demo:** the second floor's elevator is a Wolfenstein 3-D-style elevator, with the
  locked switch on its end wall where you can see it.

## PFWolf Editor

- **The 3D view and the shade preview** no longer show Blake Stone's palette-cycle colors in dark
  areas.

## For mod makers

Nothing a mod is made of has changed incompatibly: everything is new fields, and mods made for
0.9.3 work as they did.

- **`game-info.yaml` `level-bonuses`:** bonuses given once a level for getting all of `kills`,
  `points`, `treasure`, `secrets` or `informants`, or `when: score` (the score reaching `at`,
  `recurs` for every multiple), `when: extra-life`, or `when: killed` (one of `actors`). Each can
  give `points`, `health` and `give` items, show a `message` (with `cluster-messages` per cluster)
  in a HUD message `style`, play a `sound`, and put a `score-text` on the score for
  `score-text-tics`. See the `blake` pack's `game-info.yaml`.
- **`game-info.yaml` also:** `extra-life-score`, `pushwall-speed` (times Wolfenstein 3-D's speed),
  `areas-mark-player` (each floor code the player steps into joins the areas that see and hear
  them, until a door moves) and `noise-carries-over` (gunfire is heard through the next tic).
- **`hud-messages.yaml` styles** take a `priority`: while a message is up, one in a style of lower
  priority at the same place isn't shown.
- **actordefs:** flags `BLOCKSTILE` (blocks the player on its own tile only, as a static thing)
  and `KEEPSOLID` (dead, it still blocks its tile); properties `barrier.damage` and
  `barrier.chance` (a barrier hurts what's alive in its tile while it's on) and
  `monster.touchtics` (the least tics between a touching monster's zaps).
- **The `blake` pack's `player.maxlives`** is now 4.

## Compatibility

- **Saves from 0.9.3 load in 0.9.4.**
- **In a multiplayer game everyone needs the same version:** 0.9.3 and 0.9.4 can't play together.

## Known issues

<!-- decide: add or remove anything you know of from testing -->
- **The programs aren't signed:** SmartScreen asks on Windows, and Gatekeeper on a Mac (see
  [The first run](#the-first-run)).
- **The editor on a Mac uses the Control key**, not Command, for its shortcuts.
- **Reverb and muffling need OpenAL's EFX**, which the bundled OpenAL Soft has. Without it the
  sounds play dry, at full treble.
- **With the uncapped frame rate,** the view and actors move smoothly, but sliding doors,
  pushwalls and the weapon bob still move in the game's 70-a-second steps.
- **Blake Stone and Planet Strike** are close to the originals, but a few behaviours still
  differ.
