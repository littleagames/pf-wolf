**PFWolf** (Project-Friendly Wolfenstein 3D) is a source port of Wolfenstein 3-D, Spear of Destiny
and Blake Stone, built so you can make a game, or change one, with data files instead of
changing the engine. It comes with **PFWolf Editor**, a map and content editor for the same files.

0.9.2 is a **bug-fix release** for the 0.9.1 beta: a renderer crash, a Wolfenstein 3-D secret
that blocked a corridor, and several sounds that didn't behave as in the original. New to
PFWolf? The [0.9.1 beta notes](https://github.com/littleagames/pf-wolf/releases/tag/v0.9.1-beta)
cover everything in it and which game data files it needs.

Each has the game, the editor and the example mods. To update, replace the old folder (or app)
with the new one. Your saves, settings and mods live in your PFWolf data folder, so they stay where
they are.

## The first run

- **Windows:** unzip anywhere and run `PFWolf.exe` (the editor is `PFWolf.Editor.exe`). The
  programs aren't signed yet, so SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": choose
  **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** untar anywhere and run `./PFWolf`; `./install-desktop-entries.sh` adds the game and
  the editor to your application menu. Or make the AppImage executable and run it: it's the game,
  and `--editor` opens the editor.
- **macOS:** unzip, then drag **PFWolf.app** and **PFWolf Editor.app** into Applications. They
  aren't notarized yet, so the first time macOS won't open them: right-click the app and choose
  **Open** (on macOS 15 and later: try once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open
  Anyway**). In the editor, choose PFWolf.app as the game folder.

## Fixed

### Playing

- **A crash while a pushwall slides east or west.** Looking at certain moving secret walls
  from the wrong angle could close the game (in Wolfenstein 3-D, E1M3's secret at 39,2).
- **Pushwalls stopping short at the map's edge.** A secret wall that should slide onto an open tile
  on the map's border stopped a tile early. In E1M3 that left the corridor behind the secret at 7,61
  blocked.
- **A pushwall that stops beside a doorway** now shows the door's side texture, as walls
  beside doors do. Before, only walls that were there when the level loaded got it (in
  Wolfenstein 3-D, E2M3's pushwall at 35,18 beside the door at 33,17).
- **Searching for secrets with Use held down.** As in the original, holding Use while you slide
  along a wall pushes any secret wall you reach. When a held push is blocked, or comes from a
  one-way pushwall's wrong side, it makes no sound and shows no message. Switches and other use
  triggers still go off once per press.
- **The elevator switch** now shows as thrown before the level ends. The game waits only for
  the switch's own sound, not for a door or gun still sounding. In Wolfenstein 3-D and Spear the
  switch plays its original sound again.

### Sound

- **A held chaingun's shots retrigger, as in the original.** They no longer pile on top of each
  other. The player's gunfire plays on a channel of its own, so it no longer cuts off other long
  sounds, and the bosses' chaingun bursts share one channel as in Wolf4SDL.
- **The level-end tally runs at the original's speed with AdLib sound effects.** The fade-out
  added to the end of each AdLib sound made the tally wait longer between steps.
- **No pop at the end of some AdLib sounds**, the knife swing among them, in both the game and
  the editor.
- **The menu music no longer skips back** each time you move the cursor in Customize Controls.

### PFWolf Editor

- **The 3D view shows enemies from the side facing the camera**, as the game does. Before, it always
  showed their front.

### Other

- **The Join Game screen's "Looking for games"** now animates while it searches.

## For mod makers

Nothing a mod is made of has changed incompatibly. One thing was added and one sound changed:

- **`sounds/sound-seq.yaml` entries can name a `channel`.** All sounds that name the same
  channel play on one voice, and each new one cuts off the last, so a burst of fire
  retriggers instead of piling up. Entries without a channel take any free voice, as before. The
  built-in boss attacks (`hans/attack`, `hitler/attack`, `trans/attack`, `will/attack`,
  `uber/attack`, `knight/attack`) use `channel: boss`.
- **`switches/elevbutn` plays `DSSWITCH`** as its digitized sound, in place of `DSPLATFM`.

A mod with its own `sounds/sound-seq.yaml` replaces the game's whole file, so a copy made from
0.9.1 doesn't get these two changes. Add them to your copy to match 0.9.2.

## Compatibility

- **Saves from 0.9.1 load in 0.9.2.**
- **In a multiplayer game everyone needs the same version:** 0.9.1 and 0.9.2 can't play
  together.
- **Demos recorded in 0.9.1** may play back differently where the player pushed a secret wall,
  because the pushwall fixes change what happens there.

## Known issues

<!-- decide: add or remove anything you know of from testing -->
- **The Linux and macOS builds are new.** They build and pass the automated tests, but few people
  have played them yet.
- **The programs aren't signed:** SmartScreen asks on Windows, and Gatekeeper on a Mac (see
  [The first run](#the-first-run)).
- **The editor on a Mac uses the Control key**, not Command, for its shortcuts.
- **With the uncapped frame rate,** the view and actors move smoothly, but sliding doors,
  pushwalls and the weapon bob still move in the game's 70-a-second steps.
- **Blake Stone and Planet Strike** are close to the originals, but a few behaviours still
  differ.
